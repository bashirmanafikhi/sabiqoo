Privacy Policy — Sabiqoo

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

Sabiqoo ("the app", "we", "us") is a catalog of good deeds with lifetime tracking, developed by Bashir Manafikhi. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

2. Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDKs. All data you enter — such as deeds, counts, and history — is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it.

3. Permissions

The app requests no permissions that access personal information (no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage access).

4. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising networks, analytics providers, or any other third-party services that process your data.

5. Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data from your device settings. There is no server-side copy to delete.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the policy date above before releasing that version.
